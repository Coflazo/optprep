# Privacy notice

Effective 2 October 2026.

OptPrep is built to know nothing about you. This notice explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and when.

## What OptPrep stores

Your progress (answers, scores, lessons, streak, settings and wrong-answer history) is saved in your own browser's storage on your device. It never reaches Coflazo or any server run for OptPrep. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts. Fonts and icons ship with the app, so loading a page does not contact anyone else.

If you run the optional local server from the source code, your progress is also saved in a database file on your own computer.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose one of these:

- **Export a backup.** OptPrep saves a file to your device. You decide where it goes.
- **Sync with GitHub.** If you paste a GitHub token in Settings, OptPrep stores your progress in one secret gist in your own GitHub account and talks only to `api.github.com`. The token stays in your browser. With a passphrase, OptPrep encrypts the file before uploading it. GitHub handles that data under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Disconnect in Settings to stop syncing, and delete the gist on GitHub to remove the copy.

GitHub Pages, which hosts the online version, may log technical data such as IP addresses when you load the site. GitHub controls those logs, not OptPrep.

## Your rights

Coflazo never receives your progress, so there is no personal data held about you to access, correct or delete. You control all of it: reset or export it in Settings, or clear the site's data in your browser. Under the GDPR you keep the right to complain to a data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## Children

OptPrep is meant for university students and graduates. It is not aimed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. It collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes

If OptPrep ever starts to send data anywhere, this notice will change first and the app will ask before doing it. The effective date above shows the latest version.
